FIELD MANUAL

👽 On foot: Zap-O-Matic never runs out. Disintegrator lobs plasma that melts groups. PK grabs humans, cows, cars & soldiers — release RMB to hurl them.
🛸 Saucer: LMB = Death Ray (melts buildings/tanks). Hold RMB = Abducto-beam (sucks up humans & cows for bonus DNA). Q = shockwave that flings everything. F = land & exit.
🧬 DNA heals you slightly and is your score. Abduction gives 2× DNA vs killing.
🚨 Alert: each atrocity raises the alert. Army sends GIs → cops → tanks → AA flak → Majestic agents. Lay low or fight through.
💡 Tip: your saucer is parked in the pasture south of town. Walk to it and press F. Cows give easy early DNA.
❤️ Survival: shields regen. If on-foot health hits 0 you auto-recall to the saucer. If the saucer hull hits 0 — game over.
